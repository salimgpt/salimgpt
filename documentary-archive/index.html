LONG-TERM DISCOVERY

Documentary discovery should extend beyond the latest release

New documentaries naturally receive attention when they are published, but older work can remain useful for research, context and subject discovery. The archive provides a route back into that published body of work.

Instead of treating older documentaries as obsolete, SalimGPT can connect them with topics, series, collections and other discovery pathways where those relationships are relevant.

ARCHIVE CONTEXT

How the archive supports discovery

The archive is a discovery layer for published documentary work rather than a fabricated chronological catalogue or automatically generated ranking.

01

Previously published work

The archive is intended to help audiences return to documentaries that have already been published within SalimGPT language editions.

02

Multiple discovery paths

Archived documentaries may also be discoverable through topics, documentary series, collections, featured pathways or other relevant navigation.

03

Edition-specific history

Each language edition can have a different publication history, so the contents of one archive should not be assumed to match another edition exactly.

04

No invented catalogue data

Documentary titles, publication dates, archive counts and classifications should be presented only when they are supported by the actual published catalogue.
