Privacy at SalimGPT

SalimGPT is an independent documentary media brand. The global SalimGPT website is primarily designed to provide information about the brand, its editorial standards, language editions, policies and official destinations.

You do not need to create a SalimGPT account simply to browse the public global website.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the SalimGPT global website located at salimgpt.github.io/salimgpt/ and to information voluntarily submitted through SalimGPT contact, correction, copyright or similar communication channels made available through this website.

Individual SalimGPT language editions, external platforms and third-party services may operate under additional or separate privacy practices.

2. Information You Provide Voluntarily

SalimGPT may receive personal information when you voluntarily contact us or submit a request through a form or communication channel.

Depending on the type of request, this information may include:

  • Your name or preferred name.
  • Your email address or other contact information that you choose to provide.
  • The content of your message.
  • Information included in a correction request, copyright request or other inquiry.
  • Supporting links, references or documents that you voluntarily provide.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for the purpose of your request.

3. Technical Information

When you access a website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the hosting infrastructure, browser, network provider or other services involved in delivering the website.

This may include information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Approximate technical location derived from an IP address.
  • Requested pages and referring pages.
  • Date and time of requests.
  • Technical error, security or diagnostic information.

The SalimGPT global website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Technical processing performed by the hosting provider is subject to the provider’s own systems and applicable policies.

4. Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

The SalimGPT global website may use browser storage or similar technologies when needed for essential website functionality, preferences, accessibility or future site features.

Third-party platforms that you visit from SalimGPT may independently use cookies, analytics, advertising technologies or account identifiers under their own privacy policies.

You can manage cookies and stored website data using the privacy controls provided by your browser.

5. How Information May Be Used

Information associated with the SalimGPT global website may be used for legitimate operational, editorial, security and communication purposes, including to:

  • Operate, maintain and improve the website.
  • Respond to questions or other communications.
  • Review correction requests.
  • Review copyright or content-use requests.
  • Investigate website errors, abuse or security issues.
  • Protect SalimGPT, its original work, users and website infrastructure.
  • Maintain records when reasonably necessary for editorial, administrative or legal purposes.

6. Third-Party Services & Platforms

SalimGPT may provide links to or use infrastructure associated with third-party services. Examples may include website hosting, social media platforms, video platforms or other official SalimGPT destinations.

These services may process information independently under their own privacy policies, terms and technical systems.

Visiting an external website or platform means your interaction may become subject to that third party’s policies rather than this Privacy Policy.

7. Information Sharing

SalimGPT does not treat personal information submitted through the global website as a product to be sold.

Information may nevertheless be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Use service providers that support website or communication operations.
  • Respond to a request you initiated.
  • Protect legal rights, safety, security or intellectual property.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations, enforceable requests or lawful process.
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents or misuse of SalimGPT services.

8. Data Retention

Information voluntarily submitted to SalimGPT may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain appropriate records, protect legal rights, resolve disputes or satisfy applicable obligations.

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

9. Information Security

SalimGPT seeks to use reasonable organizational and technical practices appropriate to the nature of the website and the information being handled.

However, no internet transmission, hosting platform, storage system or communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children’s Privacy

The global SalimGPT website is a public documentary media and information website and is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children.

Users should not submit personal information about a child unless they have an appropriate and lawful reason to do so.

11. International Users

SalimGPT is intended for a multilingual and international audience. Website infrastructure and third-party services may process information in countries different from the country in which a visitor is located.

Applicable privacy rights and legal requirements may vary depending on jurisdiction.

12. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights concerning personal information, such as requesting access, correction, deletion or information about how certain data is handled.

These rights are not identical in every jurisdiction and may be subject to legal exceptions.

You may also control certain browser-based information through your browser’s privacy, cookie and storage settings.

13. External Links

SalimGPT pages may contain links to language editions, YouTube, social media profiles, reference sources or other websites.

SalimGPT does not control the privacy practices of independent external websites. Review the privacy information provided by those services before submitting personal information to them.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised as SalimGPT develops, new website features are introduced or legal and operational requirements change.

When this page is materially updated, the “Last updated” date near the top of the page should also be revised.

15. Contact SalimGPT

If you have a privacy-related question concerning SalimGPT or information you submitted through the global website, use the official contact page.
