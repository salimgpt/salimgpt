About This Disclaimer

SalimGPT publishes research-based documentary and editorial media for informational, educational and documentary purposes.

SalimGPT seeks to use reliable sources, appropriate context and human editorial review, but the existence of research and review does not mean that every statement, source, interpretation or external resource can be guaranteed to remain complete, current or free from error.

1. General Information

Information published through the SalimGPT global website and official SalimGPT documentary channels is provided in good faith for documentary, educational and informational purposes.

The content should be considered in its full context, including the sources, dates, qualifications and limitations relevant to the subject being discussed.

2. Documentary & Editorial Purpose

SalimGPT may cover history, society, science, technology, human civilization, investigations, mystery, current affairs and other documentary subjects.

Some topics may involve disputed accounts, incomplete historical records, developing events, scientific uncertainty, conflicting sources or competing interpretations.

Documentary presentation of a claim, allegation, viewpoint, historical account or public controversy does not automatically mean that SalimGPT adopts that material as an established fact.

3. Research, Sources & Evidence

SalimGPT aims to develop its documentary content through research, source evaluation, contextual analysis and editorial review.

Sources may include books, academic publications, official records, reports, reputable journalism, archival materials, public statements, databases, expert commentary and other relevant materials.

Different sources may vary in reliability, methodology, date, scope and perspective. Where appropriate, SalimGPT may identify uncertainty, disagreement or limitations in the available evidence.

4. Accuracy, Completeness & Timeliness

SalimGPT seeks to publish accurate and responsibly reviewed material. However, information may later become incomplete, outdated, disputed or incorrect as new evidence, records, research or developments emerge.

Publication does not create a guarantee that every statement, statistic, quotation, translation, interpretation or external source will remain permanently accurate or current.

Content may be corrected, clarified, updated, supplemented or removed when appropriate.

5. Interpretation, Analysis & Context

Documentary storytelling sometimes requires summarizing complex evidence, presenting historical context, comparing sources or explaining competing interpretations.

Analytical or explanatory passages should not be confused with primary-source evidence. Where relevant, SalimGPT may distinguish between documented facts, attributed claims, interpretation and uncertainty.

Viewers and readers are encouraged to consult referenced primary or authoritative sources when a subject requires additional verification or deeper investigation.

6. Human-Led, AI-Assisted Production

SalimGPT may use artificial intelligence as an assistance tool during parts of the production workflow.

AI-assisted tasks may include research organization, drafting support, translation support, visual development, narration support, production planning or other workflow activities.

SalimGPT is not an AI chatbot or general-purpose AI service. Editorial judgment, source verification, fact-checking, creative direction, review and final publication decisions remain human-led.

Certain documentary productions may use illustrations, animation, reconstruction, generated visuals, synthetic narration or other representational techniques where appropriate to the storytelling context.

Such material should not automatically be interpreted as original photographic or audiovisual documentation of the depicted event.

Where the distinction is materially important, SalimGPT may provide contextual disclosure or other information about the nature of the media used.

SalimGPT documentary and informational content is not intended to replace individualized advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

When a decision requires professional assessment, users should consult a suitably qualified expert who can consider the specific circumstances involved.
