ABOUT SALIMGPT

Identity & Purpose

Questions about what SalimGPT is, what the name represents and how the documentary brand is defined.

What is SalimGPT?

SalimGPT is an independent, research-based, multilingual documentary media brand. It develops documentary work around research, evidence, storytelling and multilingual audience access.

Technology and AI-assisted tools can support parts of production, while editorial judgment, source verification, fact-checking, review, creative direction and final approval remain human-led.

About SalimGPT → Mission & Vision →
Is SalimGPT an AI chatbot or AI model?

No. SalimGPT is a documentary media brand, not an AI chatbot or an AI model.

AI can assist parts of its research, writing, visual, language and production workflow, but SalimGPT itself is a human-led media project.

Why “SalimGPT”? → Human-Led, AI-Assisted →
Why is “GPT” part of the SalimGPT name?

“Salim” refers to founder Mohammad Salim. “GPT” is retained in the name because AI-assisted tools play a substantial supporting role in parts of the documentary production workflow.

The name does not mean that SalimGPT is an AI system. Editorial responsibility and final creative decisions remain human-led.

Read the Name Explanation → Founder & Director →
What subjects can SalimGPT documentaries cover?

SalimGPT documentary subjects are not restricted to a fixed list of categories. A documentary can examine a broad field, a narrow issue, a local question, an emerging subject, an interdisciplinary topic or a highly specific researchable question.

The topic pages on the global site function as broad discovery gateways rather than boundaries on what SalimGPT can research.

LANGUAGES & VIEWING

Multilingual Access

Questions about language editions, YouTube audio and where audiences can discover SalimGPT documentary media.

Which SalimGPT language editions are available?

Bengali, English and Hindi are currently available as dedicated SalimGPT language editions.

Additional language editions can be introduced as multilingual editorial and production capacity expands.

Language Editions → Explore Languages →
Does SalimGPT use a separate YouTube channel for every language?

SalimGPT's main YouTube structure is centered on one primary channel rather than a separate primary YouTube channel for each language.

Where supported and available, additional language audio tracks can provide multilingual access to the same documentary video.

YouTube Multi-Language Audio →
Does every documentary have every audio language?

Not necessarily. Audio-language availability can differ between documentaries according to localization status, production readiness and editorial review.

The existence of a SalimGPT language edition does not automatically mean that every documentary already includes an audio track in that language.

How Multi-Language Audio Works →

RESEARCH & EDITORIAL

Evidence, Review & AI

Questions about how documentary research is handled and where AI-assisted tools fit within the production process.

How does SalimGPT approach research and evidence?

Research, source evaluation, evidence and context are treated as core parts of documentary development.

The exact research process can vary by subject, but SalimGPT provides dedicated methodology and source-standard pages describing how evidence is approached within the editorial workflow.

Research Methodology → Source & Evidence Standards →
Does SalimGPT fact-check documentary content?

Fact-checking and human review form part of SalimGPT's editorial workflow. Claims should be examined in relation to their supporting sources, context and available evidence.

Fact-Checking → Human Review Standard →
Does SalimGPT use AI to create documentaries?

AI tools may assist multiple production tasks, including research support, drafting, visual development, language work, synthetic voice and other technical or creative workflows.

AI assistance does not replace human editorial responsibility. Source verification, editorial judgment, fact-checking, review, creative direction and final approval remain human-led.

Human-Led, AI-Assisted → Visual & Synthetic Media →
Who makes the final editorial decisions?

SalimGPT's editorial judgment, review, creative direction and final approval are human-led. AI-assisted tools may support production, but they do not independently determine SalimGPT's final editorial position or publication decision.
