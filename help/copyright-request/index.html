COPYRIGHT & RIGHTS

Copyright Request

If you believe SalimGPT documentary media, a webpage, image, audio element, video segment or other published material raises a copyright concern, use this page to understand what information can help identify and review the issue.

Copyright concerns are different from factual correction requests and general questions. Provide enough information to identify the material involved, your relationship to the rights concerned and the action you are requesting.

COPYRIGHT CONCERNS

Identify the material and the type of concern

Copyright-related questions can involve different kinds of media and different rights. These examples are intended to help describe the issue clearly rather than define every possible copyright matter.

Video or Footage

A documentary segment, archival clip, recorded footage or other moving-image material may be part of the concern.

Image or Graphic

A photograph, illustration, map, graphic, thumbnail or another visual element may be involved.

Audio or Music

Music, recorded sound, narration, an interview clip or another audio element may be the subject of the request.

Written Material

Text, quotations, translated material, research writing or another written work may be involved.

Design or Creative Asset

A layout element, creative asset or other protected visual work may form part of a rights concern.

Other Protected Material

Another identifiable work may require review when the copyright concern and the material involved can be described clearly.

RIGHTS REVIEW

A copyright request should identify both the work and the claimed rights

A useful copyright concern explains what protected material is involved, where it appears within SalimGPT content and why the requester believes they are authorized to raise the issue.

The appropriate response can depend on the nature of the material, available documentation, licenses, permissions, applicable exceptions and other relevant circumstances. This page does not make a legal determination about a particular claim.

PREPARE YOUR REQUEST

Make the material easy to identify

A request should point to the exact SalimGPT material involved. For a documentary, this may include a title and timestamp. For a webpage, it may include the page and the specific image, text or media element.

When relevant, provide information identifying the original work and documentation that helps explain your connection to the rights being asserted.

REQUEST CHECKLIST

Information That Can Help Identify a Copyright Concern

The exact information needed can vary. Include what is relevant to your specific concern and avoid submitting information unrelated to the issue.

01

Identify the SalimGPT material

Provide the documentary, webpage, image, audio, video or other SalimGPT material involved.

02

Identify the copyrighted work

Describe the original work that you believe is affected by the SalimGPT material.

03

Explain your relationship to the rights

State whether you are the rights holder or otherwise authorized to raise the copyright concern.

04

Point to the exact location

Include a timestamp, page section, image reference or other precise location where possible.

05

Provide supporting documentation

Include relevant information that helps establish ownership, authorization or the origin of the work.

06

State the requested action

Explain what you are asking SalimGPT to review, change, remove, clarify or otherwise address.

REVIEW WORKFLOW

How a Copyright Concern Can Be Reviewed

The exact process can vary according to the material, documentation and circumstances of the request.

01

Identify the disputed material

The exact SalimGPT content and the work identified by the requester can be located and compared.

02

Review rights information

Relevant ownership information, licenses, permissions and supporting documentation can be examined.

03

Review the use in context

The way the material appears within the documentary or webpage can be considered together with the available rights information.

04

Determine the appropriate response

Based on the review, SalimGPT can determine what action, clarification or further information may be appropriate.
