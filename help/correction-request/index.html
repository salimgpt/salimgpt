EDITORIAL CORRECTIONS

Correction Request

If you believe a SalimGPT documentary, webpage or published statement contains a factual error, inaccurate detail, source problem or other issue that may require correction, use this page to understand what information is useful for editorial review.

A correction request should identify the specific material involved and explain the concern as clearly as possible. Supporting evidence is especially useful when the issue concerns a factual claim.

WHAT CAN BE REPORTED?

Correction concerns can involve different types of published information

The examples below are common reasons to request editorial review. They are not intended to limit the kinds of legitimate correction concerns that can be raised.

Factual Error

A date, number, name, location, event, quotation, description or other factual detail appears to be incorrect.

Source or Attribution Issue

A source appears to be misidentified, incorrectly attributed, insufficiently represented or connected to the wrong claim.

Context or Representation

A factual statement may require review because important context changes how the information should reasonably be understood.

Translation or Language Issue

A translated title, caption, subtitle, audio line or other localized wording may materially change the intended factual meaning.

Visual or Media Accuracy

A visual, caption, map, graphic or synthetic-media element may create an inaccurate factual representation.

Other Verifiable Concern

Another specific issue may warrant review when it can be clearly identified and evaluated against relevant evidence.

EDITORIAL REVIEW

A correction request is evaluated against evidence

A disagreement by itself does not establish that published material is factually wrong. The relevant question is whether the disputed information is supported by the available evidence, accurately represented and appropriately contextualized.

Where uncertainty or competing interpretations exist, the review should distinguish documented facts from interpretation, allegation, inference and unresolved questions.

PREPARE YOUR REQUEST

Identify exactly what you believe should be reviewed

A useful correction request points to the specific content in question rather than referring broadly to an entire documentary or page.

If possible, identify the exact statement, timestamp, paragraph, caption, image, translation or other element and explain why you believe it may be inaccurate.

REQUEST CHECKLIST

Information That Can Help With Review

Not every case requires every item below, but providing relevant detail can make the issue easier to locate and evaluate.

01

Identify the content

Provide the documentary title, webpage, language edition or other SalimGPT material involved.

02

Point to the exact issue

Include a timestamp, quotation, paragraph, caption, image or other precise location when possible.

03

Explain the concern

Describe what appears inaccurate and what you believe requires verification or correction.

04

Provide supporting evidence

Identify reliable sources, records or other evidence that directly supports the correction concern.

05

Suggest the accurate information

When possible, explain what the corrected information should say and why the available evidence supports it.

06

Keep the request focused

Separate distinct issues where possible so each factual concern can be evaluated on its own evidence.

REVIEW WORKFLOW

How a Correction Concern Can Be Examined

The exact review may vary according to the issue, the evidence available and the type of published material.

01

Locate the disputed material

The specific documentary segment, webpage text, visual, source reference or localized content is identified.

02

Recheck the evidence

Relevant sources and supporting material can be compared with the published claim or representation.

03

Review context and wording

The review can consider whether the information is technically accurate but presented without necessary context or precision.

04

Determine whether an update is warranted

If the review establishes a material issue, the relevant published content can be corrected, clarified or updated as appropriate.
