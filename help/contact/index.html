BEFORE YOU SEND A REQUEST

Clear context makes a request easier to understand

When contacting SalimGPT about a specific documentary, page or published statement, identify the exact material you are referring to and explain the purpose of your message clearly.

For correction or copyright matters, include the information requested on the dedicated request page rather than submitting only a general description.

USEFUL INFORMATION

What to Include When Relevant

Not every inquiry needs every detail, but these elements can help identify the subject of a request and reduce unnecessary back-and-forth.

01

Identify the relevant content

Provide the documentary title, page name, section or other specific SalimGPT material involved.

02

Explain your request clearly

State what you are asking, reporting or requesting without requiring the reviewer to infer the purpose.

03

Include supporting context

When evidence or documentation is relevant, identify the supporting source or contextual information.

04

Use the correct request pathway

Correction and copyright matters should use their dedicated pages so the necessary details can be reviewed in context.
