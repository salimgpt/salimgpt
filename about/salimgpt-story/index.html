OUR STORY

The SalimGPT Story

SalimGPT is built around a simple idea: documentary storytelling can use modern technology without handing editorial responsibility over to technology.

The result is a research-based multilingual documentary media brand where AI can assist parts of production, while human judgment, verification, creative direction, review and final approval remain central.

THE BEGINNING

A media identity built around documentary work

SalimGPT was conceived as a documentary media identity rather than an AI product. Its purpose is to support research-driven storytelling across subjects that can benefit from deeper context, evidence and structured explanation.

The name connects the founder with the role of modern AI-assisted production. “Salim” comes from founder Mohammad Salim, while “GPT” reflects the use of artificial intelligence as an assisting technology within parts of the workflow.

That distinction remains important: SalimGPT is not an AI model or chatbot. It is a documentary media brand whose editorial decisions remain human-led.

THE FOUNDATION

Four ideas shape the SalimGPT approach

The project brings research, human editorial responsibility, AI-assisted production and multilingual publishing into one documentary workflow.

01

Research First

Documentary development begins with questions, evidence, sources and context rather than treating production tools as substitutes for research.

02

Human Editorial Control

Humans remain responsible for evaluating evidence, shaping the narrative, reviewing factual claims and approving what is finally published.

03

AI as an Assistant

Artificial intelligence can support research, drafting, language work, visual development and other production tasks without becoming the final editorial authority.

04

Multilingual Reach

Dedicated language editions allow documentary work to reach different audiences while keeping language and publishing context organized.

THE MODEL

Technology became part of the workflow, not the editor

Modern production tools can make parts of documentary work faster and more flexible. AI can assist with research organization, early drafting, visual development, language and localization, synthetic media and other production tasks where appropriate.

SalimGPT’s model separates that assistance from editorial authority. Human judgment remains responsible for deciding what evidence is credible, what context is necessary, how a subject should be framed and whether material is ready to publish.

FROM IDEA TO DOCUMENTARY

The story continues through the editorial process

The production model is designed around a sequence of human decisions supported by tools rather than a fully automated publishing pipeline.

1
SUBJECT

Identify a researchable documentary question

A documentary begins with a subject, question, problem, event or idea that can be explored through evidence and meaningful context.

2
RESEARCH

Gather and assess sources

Research can involve locating sources, comparing evidence, identifying uncertainty and developing the context needed to understand the subject.

3
DEVELOPMENT

Build the documentary narrative

Research is shaped into a coherent structure through human editorial judgment, with AI tools able to assist selected drafting or production tasks.

4
REVIEW

Check facts, context and presentation

Claims, sources, language, visual choices and production outputs require human review before final publication decisions are made.

5
PUBLICATION

Publish for the relevant audience

Documentary content can be distributed through language editions and supported channels while the global site connects audiences with the wider SalimGPT platform.

MULTILINGUAL BY DESIGN

The project is structured to travel across languages

SalimGPT Global serves as the central brand, discovery, trust and language gateway. Dedicated language editions provide separate environments for documentary publishing.

Bengali, English and Hindi editions are available, while additional languages can be introduced as the platform expands.

WHAT REMAINS HUMAN

Editorial responsibility stays with people

Tools may change and production methods may evolve, but the core editorial responsibilities of the project remain human-led.

Research Judgment

Deciding what to investigate and how evidence should be interpreted remains a human responsibility.

Fact-Checking

Claims require review and verification rather than automatic acceptance of generated output.

Creative Direction

Narrative choices, framing and the creative identity of documentary work remain human-led.

Final Approval

The decision to approve and publish documentary material remains under human editorial control.

A CONTINUING STORY

The platform can evolve while its editorial principle stays clear

SalimGPT can expand into new subjects, formats, languages and production methods as the documentary platform develops.

That expansion does not require abandoning the distinction at the center of the project: technology can assist the work, while humans remain responsible for editorial judgment and publication.
