FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Mohammad Salim

Founder and director of SalimGPT, responsible for the human editorial direction behind the research-based, multilingual documentary media project.

SalimGPT may use artificial intelligence throughout selected parts of production, but research judgment, source verification, fact-checking, creative direction, editorial review and final approval remain human-led.

THE ROLE

Founder responsibility extends beyond the creation of the brand

Mohammad Salim established SalimGPT as a documentary media identity built around research, evidence, multilingual publishing and the responsible use of modern production technology.

As director, the role includes setting the editorial direction of the project and maintaining the distinction between tool-assisted production and human editorial authority.

AI systems and other tools may contribute to parts of research, drafting, language work, visual development and production. They do not replace responsibility for deciding what should be investigated, how evidence is interpreted or whether material is ready to publish.

ROLE Founder & Director

Editorial direction, research oversight, creative direction, review and final publication responsibility.

EDITORIAL RESPONSIBILITIES

Responsibilities within the SalimGPT model

The director’s role connects research, editorial judgment, production and publication while keeping final responsibility with a human decision-maker.

01

Editorial Direction

Define the documentary direction, publishing priorities and editorial framework of the SalimGPT project.

02

Research Direction

Determine research questions, subject framing and the evidence required to develop a documentary responsibly.

03

Source Verification

Maintain human oversight of source assessment and the evidence used to support documentary claims and context.

04

Creative Direction

Guide narrative structure, presentation, visual direction and the creative identity of documentary work.

05

Human Review

Review research and production outputs for accuracy, context, clarity and consistency with editorial standards.

06

Final Approval

Retain human responsibility for final publication decisions rather than delegating approval to automated systems.

HUMAN-LED, AI-ASSISTED

Technology supports the workflow; it does not own the decision

SalimGPT can use artificial intelligence to assist research organization, drafting, language and localization, visual development, synthetic media and other production activities where appropriate.

The director remains responsible for deciding how such assistance is used, reviewing the results and determining whether the final material meets the project’s editorial expectations.

DIRECTION

Principles behind the editorial direction

The project is designed around several practical distinctions that shape how documentary work is researched, produced and reviewed.

Research Before Assertion

Documentary claims should be developed from research and evidence rather than from unsupported assumptions.

Verification Before Publication

Generated, summarized or collected information requires human review before it can support published material.

Tools Serve Editorial Goals

Technology is selected for its usefulness to the work, not treated as a substitute for editorial responsibility.

Accountability Remains Human

Final responsibility for editorial choices and publication remains with people.

THE NAME CONNECTION

“Salim” connects the founder directly with the brand

The first part of the SalimGPT name comes from Mohammad Salim. The second part, “GPT,” reflects the substantial role AI-assisted tools can have within selected areas of the production workflow.

Together, the name represents a media model where technology can contribute to production while the human founder and editorial process remain responsible for direction, review and publication.

MULTILINGUAL DIRECTION

A single editorial identity across multiple language editions

SalimGPT Global provides the central brand, editorial, trust and discovery framework. Dedicated language editions allow documentary publishing to serve different audiences without turning each language into a separate editorial identity.

Bengali, English and Hindi editions are available, with additional language editions planned as the platform expands.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Human leadership also means human responsibility

The human-led model is not only about creative control. It also establishes responsibility for editorial decisions, corrections, source handling, transparency and the standards applied before publication.

When tools assist the work, responsibility for how those tools are used and how their outputs are reviewed remains part of the human editorial process.
