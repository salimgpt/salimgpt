THE NAME

Why “SalimGPT”?

The name reflects two parts of the project: its founder and the role artificial intelligence can play as an assistant within documentary production.

SalimGPT is a documentary media brand. It is not an AI model, chatbot or OpenAI product. Human editorial responsibility remains at the center of the work.

TWO PARTS, ONE IDENTITY

What the name represents

The name combines a human identity with a reference to the technology that can assist parts of the production process.

SALIM

The founder

“Salim” comes from founder Mohammad Salim. It represents the human origin, direction and responsibility behind the documentary media project.

The human element is not merely part of the name. Research judgment, editorial decisions, creative direction, review and final approval remain human-led.

GPT

AI-assisted production

“GPT” is retained because artificial intelligence can play a substantial assisting role in parts of SalimGPT’s production workflow.

AI may assist research, drafting, visual development, language and localization, synthetic media and other production tasks where appropriate.

AN IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The name describes the brand — not a software product

The presence of “GPT” in the name can naturally create questions about what SalimGPT is. The project is not presented as a language model, chatbot or general-purpose artificial intelligence system.

SalimGPT is an independent documentary media brand. Technology is used within production, while editorial authority remains with people.

WHY KEEP “GPT”?

Because AI assistance is part of the production reality

Removing the technology reference from the name would hide an important part of how the project can work. AI tools may contribute meaningfully to research support, drafting, language work, visual development and other stages of production.

Keeping “GPT” in the name acknowledges that assistance while making another distinction equally important: assistance does not equal editorial control.

ASSISTANCE VS. AUTHORITY

What AI may assist — and what remains human

SalimGPT’s production model distinguishes between tools that help perform work and people who remain responsible for editorial decisions.

AI MAY ASSIST

Production Support

  • Research assistance
  • Drafting assistance
  • Language and localization support
  • Visual development
  • Synthetic media where appropriate
  • Other production-support tasks
HUMANS REMAIN RESPONSIBLE

Editorial Authority

  • Research direction
  • Source verification
  • Fact-checking
  • Editorial judgment
  • Creative direction and review
  • Final publication approval

CORE PRINCIPLE

Technology can assist the work without becoming the editor

SalimGPT’s identity is built around that separation. AI can be useful within the workflow, but usefulness does not transfer responsibility for evidence, interpretation, editorial choices or publication.

Bengali, English and Hindi editions are currently available, with additional language editions planned as the platform expands.

BRAND CLARITY

SalimGPT is its own documentary media identity

The term “GPT” in the brand name describes the role AI-assisted tools can have in production. It should not be interpreted as meaning that SalimGPT itself is a GPT model or that the brand is an OpenAI product.

SalimGPT maintains its own editorial identity, documentary approach, research standards, review process and publication responsibility.
