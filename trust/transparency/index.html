Transparency

SalimGPT is an independent, research-based, multilingual documentary media brand. Transparency means explaining important parts of how documentary work is researched, developed, reviewed, produced, corrected and presented to audiences.

Transparency does not mean publishing every internal working note or production detail. It means making material editorial practices, responsibilities and distinctions understandable where they matter to the audience.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Human-led editorial responsibility remains central

SalimGPT can use AI-assisted tools extensively within production workflows, but those tools do not replace human responsibility for editorial judgment, source verification, fact-checking, review, creative direction or final approval.

When AI-assisted or synthetic processes materially affect how content is created or represented, SalimGPT's broader production and synthetic-media standards provide the relevant context.

DISCLOSURE IN CONTEXT

Transparency should explain what matters without creating false precision

Different documentaries may involve different research methods, source types, production tools, languages and creative techniques. A useful disclosure should match the actual work rather than apply a generic statement that implies every project follows an identical process.

Where a particular issue deserves more detail, SalimGPT can direct audiences to the relevant research, editorial, synthetic-media, correction or ownership standard.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Transparency depends on separating different kinds of information

Documentary communication becomes clearer when factual claims, interpretation, uncertainty and production choices are not presented as if they were the same thing.

01

Fact vs. Interpretation

Documented facts should be distinguishable from analysis, interpretation or explanatory framing.

02

Evidence vs. Allegation

A claim made by a source should not automatically be presented as an independently established fact.

03

Known vs. Uncertain

When evidence is incomplete or disputed, uncertainty should remain visible rather than being filled with unsupported certainty.

04

Human Decision vs. Tool Assistance

AI-assisted production should not be confused with who carries editorial responsibility for the final published work.

MULTILINGUAL CONTEXT

Language access can change presentation without changing editorial responsibility

SalimGPT is designed as a multilingual documentary platform. Language editions and additional audio tracks can make the same research or documentary work accessible to different audiences.

Localization can involve translation, adaptation and language-specific presentation, but those processes still require attention to factual meaning, context and human review.

TRANSPARENCY HAS CONTEXT

Transparency does not require publishing every internal detail

Internal drafts, unpublished research material, production notes, security-sensitive information, confidential communications or material restricted by legal, contractual, privacy or rights considerations may not be suitable for public disclosure.

The relevant goal is to provide enough public information for audiences to understand important editorial practices and identify where more specific standards apply.
