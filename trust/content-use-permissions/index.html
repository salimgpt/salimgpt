REUSE, ATTRIBUTION & PERMISSIONS

Content Use & Permissions

Public access to SalimGPT content does not automatically mean that every video, image, graphic, script, translation, audio track, brand asset or third-party element is available for unrestricted copying, modification or redistribution.

The appropriate approach depends on the exact material, the proposed use, any applicable license or permission, third-party rights and the context in which the content will be reused.

START WITH THE TYPE OF USE

Referencing content and republishing content are not the same thing

Different uses can raise different attribution, permission, licensing and rights considerations. The first step is to identify what you actually plan to do with the material.

Linking

Sharing a link to an official SalimGPT webpage or documentary is different from copying and republishing the underlying work.

Limited Quotation or Reference

A limited quotation used for commentary, reference or discussion is different from reproducing a substantial part of the original work.

Reproduction

Copying complete or substantial portions of videos, scripts, graphics, images or other creative work can require a separate rights review.

Adaptation

Editing, transforming, reformatting or building a new work from SalimGPT material can involve rights beyond ordinary reference or linking.

Translation & Localization

Translating or localizing a documentary, article, transcript, subtitle or graphic can create an adapted version of the underlying work.

Commercial or Promotional Use

Use within advertising, paid products, commercial services or promotional material can require additional consideration of permission and rights.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Permission should match the exact material and the exact proposed use

A permission that applies to one image, one platform, one language or one production does not automatically extend to every other asset or use. The relevant material, purpose, format, audience and scope should be identified as clearly as possible.

This is especially important when a SalimGPT work includes third-party material that may have separate licensing or ownership conditions.

BEFORE REUSING MATERIAL

Identify the asset before deciding how it can be reused

A documentary can contain original SalimGPT material, licensed media, quotations, archival sources, third-party images, public records, music, graphics and AI-assisted production elements. Those components may not share the same rights status.

Before substantial reuse, determine which exact element you want to use, where it came from and whether SalimGPT is in a position to authorize that specific use.

WHEN TO REVIEW PERMISSION

These uses can require closer rights review

The examples below are practical categories for identifying higher-risk reuse. They do not determine the legal status of every individual situation.

01

Republishing complete videos

Reuploading a full documentary or substantial portion of one onto another channel, website, platform or distribution service.

02

Reproducing scripts or articles

Copying substantial written material into another publication, website, book, course, database or commercial product.

03

Creating translated versions

Publishing translated scripts, subtitles, audio tracks or localized adaptations outside the official SalimGPT language structure.

04

Modifying visual assets

Editing, cropping, recoloring, remixing or repackaging branded graphics, illustrations or other creative assets for republication.

05

Commercial use

Incorporating SalimGPT material into paid products, advertising, monetized services, commercial courses or promotional campaigns.

06

Brand or identity use

Using the SalimGPT logo, wordmark or visual identity in a way that could imply an official relationship, partnership, endorsement or ownership.

ATTRIBUTION

Credit should be clear, accurate and not imply false affiliation

When attribution is appropriate, it should identify SalimGPT accurately and, where practical, point users toward the official source. Attribution should not be presented in a way that suggests SalimGPT sponsors, endorses or officially participates in an unrelated project unless that relationship actually exists.

Attribution also does not automatically replace any permission, licensing or third-party rights requirements that may apply to the material.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

SalimGPT may not control reuse rights for every element it publishes

A documentary or webpage can include material from other creators, archives, institutions, platforms or rights holders. Permission from SalimGPT cannot automatically substitute for permission that may be required from another rights holder.

Photos & Footage

Images and footage can belong to photographers, archives, agencies, institutions or other creators.

Music & Audio

Audio components can be subject to separate ownership, licensing and distribution conditions.

Documents & Archives

Historical records, documents and archival items can carry conditions independent of SalimGPT's own editorial work.

Embedded or Platform Content

Embedded material can remain governed by the platform's terms and the rights of the original publisher or creator.

AI & SYNTHETIC MEDIA

AI-assisted creation does not automatically mean unrestricted reuse

SalimGPT may use AI-assisted tools in visual development, drafting, translation, synthetic voice or other production stages. The presence of AI in the workflow does not by itself establish the rights status of every input, output or finished asset.

Reuse questions should still consider human creative contribution, source provenance, third-party material, platform terms and any applicable licensing or permission conditions.

BRAND USE

Do not create a false impression of an official SalimGPT property

SalimGPT's canonical logo and brand identity should not be modified or used in a way that makes an unrelated page, channel, product or service appear to be an official SalimGPT property.

Preserve the Canonical Logo

Do not redraw, recolor, distort, simplify or create altered versions of the canonical SalimGPT logo.

Avoid False Endorsement

References to SalimGPT should not imply sponsorship, partnership or endorsement that has not been established.

Distinguish Official Sources

Official SalimGPT properties should remain clearly distinguishable from fan pages, commentary, mirrors or unrelated third-party projects.

Ask When the Use Is Unclear

If a proposed use could create confusion about affiliation or official status, use the contact pathway before proceeding.

PREPARING A REQUEST

A useful permission request should identify the proposed use clearly

Providing specific information can make it easier to understand what is being requested and whether SalimGPT is able to authorize the material involved.

01

Identify the exact content

Include the documentary, webpage, image, clip, paragraph, graphic or other asset you want to use.

02

Explain how it will be used

Describe whether the material will be quoted, reproduced, translated, edited, embedded, distributed or incorporated into another work.

03

Identify the destination

State the website, channel, publication, platform, course, product or other destination where the material would appear.

04

Describe the audience and scope

Include relevant information about language, geographic reach, expected audience or distribution scale.

05

Explain any commercial context

Identify whether the proposed use is connected to advertising, paid access, monetization, sales or another commercial activity.

06

Identify planned modifications

Explain whether the content will be cropped, translated, edited, shortened, remixed, redesigned or otherwise changed.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

This page is general guidance, not individualized legal advice

Copyright, licensing, quotation, exceptions, attribution, transformation and permissible reuse can depend on the exact material, the proposed use, contractual terms, platform rules and applicable legal context.

SalimGPT cannot determine the legal status of every third-party work or every possible reuse scenario through a general website page. When a specific use carries material legal uncertainty, independent professional advice may be appropriate.
