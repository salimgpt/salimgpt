OWNERSHIP, RIGHTS & RESPONSIBILITY

Ownership & Copyright

SalimGPT documentaries can combine original research, writing, editorial structure, narration, visual work, licensed or authorized material, public resources, third-party sources and AI-assisted production processes. Ownership and copyright therefore need to be understood in relation to the specific material involved.

The presence of material within a SalimGPT documentary or webpage does not automatically mean that every underlying element is owned by SalimGPT or available for unrestricted reuse.

RIGHTS CAN DIFFER BY MATERIAL

Different parts of a documentary can have different rights

Documentary production can involve multiple kinds of creative and source material. Rights should therefore be considered at the level of the specific work rather than assumed from the documentary as a whole.

Original Editorial Work

Original scripts, documentary structure, written analysis, research synthesis and editorial presentation created for SalimGPT can form part of the original work associated with a production.

Original Visual & Media Assets

Original illustrations, graphics, thumbnails, edited media, motion elements or other creative assets may have their own rights and production history.

Third-Party Material

Photographs, footage, audio, quotations, documents, archives or other material created by third parties can remain subject to rights held outside SalimGPT.

Licensed or Authorized Material

Permission to use material within one production does not necessarily mean that the same material can be separately redistributed or reused by others.

Brand Assets

SalimGPT logos, wordmarks, branded graphics and related identity assets should not be treated as generic public-domain design elements simply because they appear on public webpages.

Translation & Localization

Translations, subtitles, language adaptations and multilingual audio can involve additional creative work while remaining connected to the rights of the underlying source material.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Publication does not erase the ownership history of underlying material

A documentary may combine original SalimGPT material with elements obtained from archives, contributors, licensed collections, public repositories or other sources. Publishing those elements together does not automatically create a single identical rights status for every component.

When ownership, licensing or permission matters, users should consider the specific asset involved and the conditions under which it appears.

MIXED-RIGHTS PRODUCTIONS

One documentary can contain several categories of material

Research-based documentaries often bring together material created at different times, by different people and under different conditions. A single video may therefore include original narration, licensed footage, quotations, public records, maps, archival material, graphics and AI-assisted production elements.

The presence of these materials within one finished documentary should not be interpreted as evidence that every component has the same ownership, licensing or reuse status.

RIGHTS CONTEXT

Material can enter a production through different rights pathways

These categories are general editorial distinctions, not legal classifications for every jurisdiction or every individual work.

01

Original SalimGPT Material

Material created specifically for SalimGPT can include original research synthesis, writing, narration, visual design, editing and other production elements.

02

Licensed or Permission-Based Material

Some material may be used under permission, license or another authorization that applies to a particular use or production context.

03

Openly Available or Public Resources

Some sources may be available under open licenses, public-access arrangements or other terms that permit specified forms of use.

04

Third-Party Protected Material

Material belonging to another creator or rights holder may remain subject to separate rights even when incorporated into a larger documentary work.

05

Platform-Hosted or Embedded Material

Content displayed through a third-party platform can remain subject to that platform's terms and the rights of the underlying creator or rights holder.

06

Material Requiring Further Rights Review

If the ownership or permission status of a specific asset is unclear, further review may be appropriate before reuse, redistribution or republication.

AI-ASSISTED PRODUCTION

AI assistance does not remove the need to review rights and provenance

SalimGPT may use AI systems within research, drafting, visual development, localization, synthetic voice and other production processes. The use of an AI tool does not by itself establish that every input or output is free of third-party rights or available for unrestricted reuse.

Relevant questions can include how source material was obtained, what permissions apply, what human creative work was added and whether a particular asset raises ownership, attribution or licensing concerns.

BRAND IDENTITY

SalimGPT brand assets should remain identifiable and unaltered

The SalimGPT logo and related identity assets represent the documentary media brand. Their public visibility does not imply permission to modify them, create confusing variants or present another project as an official SalimGPT property.

Logo

The canonical SalimGPT logo should not be redrawn, recolored, distorted or represented as a different mark.

Wordmark & Name

Use of the SalimGPT name or wordmark should not create a false impression of official ownership, endorsement or affiliation.

Official Identity

Official SalimGPT webpages and profiles should be distinguished from unofficial copies, mirrors, impersonations or unrelated projects.

Permission Questions

Requests involving reuse of brand material should be treated separately from ordinary linking, quotation or reference to SalimGPT.

CONTENT REUSE

Viewing or sharing a link is different from copying the underlying content

Public access to a SalimGPT webpage or documentary does not automatically establish permission to copy, download, redistribute, translate, adapt, repackage or commercially reuse every part of the material.

Before substantial reuse, consider whether permission, a specific license, attribution requirement or another applicable basis is needed for the exact content involved.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

Copyright questions can depend on the specific work and circumstances

Ownership, copyright, licensing, exceptions, attribution requirements and permissible reuse can depend on the material, source, agreement, platform and applicable legal context. A general website page cannot determine the legal status of every individual asset or use.

This page explains SalimGPT's general approach to ownership and rights information. It is not individualized legal advice.
