DOCUMENTARY TOPIC

Investigations

Explore documentary investigations built around records, evidence, data, source comparison and questions that require deeper examination beyond surface-level accounts.

Investigations is a broad discovery gateway rather than a category limited to crime, scandal or public controversy. A SalimGPT documentary may investigate a large institutional system or focus on one document, dataset, technical process, historical inconsistency, local issue, disputed claim or highly specific question.

FOLLOWING THE EVIDENCE

Investigation can begin with one unanswered detail

Investigative documentary research can examine broad systems or begin with a single inconsistency, record, claim, dataset, transaction, technical issue or missing piece of information. The areas below are examples, not a complete classification.

Documents & Records

Reports, archives, official records, correspondence, filings and other documents that can clarify events, decisions or competing accounts.

Data & Patterns

Numerical records, datasets, timelines and recurring patterns that may reveal relationships or questions not immediately visible in individual cases.

Claims & Conflicting Accounts

Statements, allegations, explanations and competing accounts that require source comparison and careful separation of evidence from assertion.

Institutions & Systems

Organizational processes, decision-making systems, procedures and institutional relationships that may require deeper documentary examination.

Technical & Process Questions

Systems, technologies, failures, procedures and technical processes whose operation or outcome requires evidence-based examination.

Specific Investigative Questions

One document, discrepancy, timeline gap, transaction, statement or narrowly defined question can provide the basis for a complete documentary investigation.

NOT LIMITED TO THESE AREAS

Any researchable question requiring deeper examination may qualify

These examples support discovery, but they do not define the limits of SalimGPT's investigative documentaries. Investigations may involve history, science, technology, society, economics, institutions, local issues or subjects that do not fit neatly inside an existing topic category.

VERIFY, COMPARE & TRACE

Investigation means testing a question against available evidence

Investigative documentary research may involve primary documents, official records, datasets, archived material, academic research, technical documentation, reporting, interviews, testimony and other sources appropriate to the subject.

Sources may conflict, records may be incomplete and some claims may remain unverified. SalimGPT therefore aims to distinguish documented facts, attributed claims, analysis, interpretation and unresolved questions instead of treating them as equivalent.

INVESTIGATIVE APPROACH

A conclusion should not run ahead of the evidence

Investigative work can involve incomplete information, disputed claims and strong competing narratives. The research process therefore needs clear separation between what can be established and what remains uncertain.

01

Define the question

Identify what is actually being investigated before collecting material or drawing conclusions.

02

Trace evidence to its source

Where possible, examine original records and identify how information moved from primary material into later reporting or claims.

03

Compare relevant accounts

Conflicting evidence and relevant alternative explanations should be examined rather than removed simply because they complicate the story.

04

Preserve uncertainty

When the evidence cannot establish a definitive answer, the documentary should make that limitation clear instead of manufacturing certainty.
